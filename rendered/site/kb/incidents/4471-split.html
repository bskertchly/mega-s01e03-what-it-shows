parser: the shared parser reads dates month first

Every tenant on file writes dates day first. The shared parser reads them month first, so a booking for 7 April lands on 4 July. This is the bug #4471 names, and the queued fix is for it. Ticket.

hint: the booking-v3 form shows the wrong order

The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, then reads what people type in the tenant’s day-first order. People do what the hint says, and the date flips before the parser sees it. The result looks the same as the parser bug, but the parser fix does not touch it. Fix the hint, or make the form read the order it shows. Ticket.

one-day move: the date moves by one day

Some reports are not a swap. The date moves by one day: Tuesday 12 May shows as Monday 11 May, a client in Lisbon gets Tuesday and a client in Toronto gets Monday, and one tenant sees it only in June. That points at time zones or daylight saving, not date order. The desk had already noted these reports (desk-notes.json), but the table on #4471 counts them under parser. Ticket.
